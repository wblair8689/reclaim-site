About Reclaim

A Mac disk space analyzer and cleaner that runs entirely on your Mac.

What Reclaim does

Reclaim shows what's filling up your Mac and helps you free up space safely, without guessing. It does one job, disk space. It isn't an antivirus or a "speed up your Mac" suite.

It is a one-time purchase. You choose the price, starting at $5, and there is no subscription. The full feature list is on the home page.

What Reclaim touches

  • It looks at the files and folders on your drive so it can show you where the space went.
  • It finds caches, logs, old installers and leftovers from deleted apps, and sorts them by how safe they are to remove.
  • It removes only what you review and confirm. By default, items go to the Trash so you can restore them. Permanent deletion only happens when you choose it.
  • iCloud offload moves files to iCloud Drive or removes local copies of files already stored there.

What Reclaim doesn't do

  • It doesn't delete anything on its own. Nothing is deleted until you review it and confirm.
  • It doesn't collect analytics, track you, or send your files or file names anywhere.
  • It doesn't use an online service to explain files. "What is this?" uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac.
  • It doesn't handle your card details. Stripe processes your payment, and we only receive your email and order details so we can send your download and support you.
