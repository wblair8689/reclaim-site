What Reclaim does

Reclaim is for when macOS says "Your disk is almost full" and you can't tell what is taking up space on your Mac. It does one job, disk space. It isn't an antivirus or a "speed up your Mac" suite.

Disk usage map

Browse your drive by folder and see exactly where the space went.

Safe cleanup

Finds caches, logs, old installers and leftovers from deleted apps, sorted by how safe they are to remove.

Large files

Lists the biggest files on your Mac so you can decide what to keep.

Unused apps

Shows apps you haven't opened in a long time, with their size, and removes them cleanly.

iCloud offload

Moves files to iCloud Drive or removes local copies of files already stored there.

What is System Data?

System Data is the catch-all category in your Mac's Storage settings. It lumps together caches, logs, temporary files and other things macOS doesn't count as apps, documents or photos, and macOS doesn't show you what's inside it. Reclaim lets you browse your drive by folder to see where the space went, and it finds caches, logs, old installers and leftovers from deleted apps, sorted by how safe they are to remove.

Why does "What is this?" need macOS 26?

"What is this?" explains unfamiliar files using Apple Intelligence on your Mac, not an online service, so your files and file names stay on your Mac. That needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence. Everything else in Reclaim works on macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
