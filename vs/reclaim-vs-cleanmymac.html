Reclaim vs CleanMyMac

Reclaim costs from $5, once. CleanMyMac starts at $3.33 a month, which is about $40 a year, every year.

The short version

CleanMyMac is an all-in-one Mac care suite: junk cleanup, malware protection, performance tools, an app uninstaller and updater, a duplicate finder and cloud cleanup, sold on a subscription. Reclaim does one job, disk space. It shows what's filling up your Mac and helps you free up space safely, and you pay for it once.

If the only thing you need is to stop the "Your disk is almost full" warning, you may not need a suite that bills you every year.

What CleanMyMac does that Reclaim doesn't

  • More than disk space. CleanMyMac scans for malware, runs performance and maintenance tasks, and updates your apps. Reclaim does none of that.
  • Duplicates and similar photos. Its Clutter module looks for duplicate files and similar photos. Reclaim lists your biggest files but doesn't look for duplicates.
  • Older Macs. CleanMyMac runs on macOS 11 or later. Reclaim needs macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
  • A free trial. You can try CleanMyMac before you pay. Reclaim has no trial, only a 30-day refund.

What Reclaim does that CleanMyMac doesn't

  • One price, once. You choose the price, starting at $5, and your purchase includes all future updates. There is no subscription to cancel.
  • Explanations. "What is this?" explains what an unfamiliar file or folder is for, using Apple Intelligence entirely on your Mac. It needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence.
  • One job, nothing else running. Reclaim only deals with disk space. Nothing is deleted until you review it and confirm, and by default items go to the Trash so you can restore them.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac. Reclaim runs entirely on your Mac. It doesn't collect analytics, track you, or send your files or file names anywhere.

Which one to pick

Pick CleanMyMac if you want one app for malware scanning, performance tools, app updates and cleanup, you are on an older version of macOS, and a yearly subscription is fine with you.

Pick Reclaim if you only want to see what is taking up space on your Mac and free it up safely, and you would rather pay once than every year.
