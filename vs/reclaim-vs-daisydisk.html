Reclaim vs DaisyDisk

Both show what's taking up space on your Mac. Both are a one-time purchase with no subscription. They differ in what happens after you see the map.

The short version

DaisyDisk shows your drive as a map and leaves the deciding to you: you find the large files and choose what to delete. Reclaim also has a disk usage map, and on top of it finds caches, logs, old installers and leftovers from deleted apps for you, sorted by how safe they are to remove.

DaisyDisk costs $9.99 once. Reclaim is pay what you want, from $5 once.

What DaisyDisk does that Reclaim doesn't

  • The ring map. DaisyDisk's map of colored rings is what it is known for, and reviewers regularly call it beautiful.
  • More places to scan. It scans external and network disks and cloud storage (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box), can scan as administrator to reach restricted folders, and can find and purge macOS snapshots.
  • A free trial. You can try DaisyDisk before you pay. Reclaim has no trial, only a 30-day refund.
  • A long track record. DaisyDisk's site shows an App Store Editors' Choice badge and a 4.7 rating from 3,796 ratings. Reclaim is new.

What Reclaim does that DaisyDisk doesn't

  • Guided safe cleanup. Reclaim finds caches, logs, old installers and leftovers from deleted apps and sorts them by how safe they are to remove. Nothing is deleted until you review it and confirm, and by default items go to the Trash so you can restore them.
  • Explanations. "What is this?" explains what an unfamiliar file or folder is for, using Apple Intelligence entirely on your Mac. It needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence.
  • Unused apps and iCloud offload. Reclaim shows apps you haven't opened in a long time, with their size, and can move files to iCloud Drive or remove local copies of files already stored there.
  • A lower starting price. You choose the price, starting at $5.

Which one to pick

Pick DaisyDisk if you want the richest map, you need to scan external, network or cloud disks, and you are comfortable working out for yourself what a folder is and whether it is safe to delete.

Pick Reclaim if your Mac says "Your disk is almost full", you want to free up space without guessing, and you would rather be shown what is safe to remove and have unfamiliar files explained before you decide.
